HISTORY

The game began in 1999. I wanted to pay tribute to Akira Toriyama for the colossal work he did in drawing the 42 volumes of the Dragon Ball series from 1984 to 1995.

The graphic style is inspired by the role-playing game Dragon Ball Z Goku Gekitōden on GameBoy:

I really liked the SD design of the characters and I was planning to create an action game. Being in high school, I started development on Ti-89 calculator, but I had to quickly drop in front of the difficulties of programming in assembler...

But I kept the idea in mind. In 2004, I transposed the basics of my game to PC thanks to Flash 5 / Actionscript. It was the birth of Dragon Ball Z Tribute. One of the first playable versions can be found on Newgrounds. This is the only time I posted the game on an external site. All the other sites that host him have stolen it. Even when they call it a "curation"... If you like this game, support the project by playing on this page, not elsewhere. Thank you.

In 2011, the game became "Dragon Ball Z Devolution". Since then, it has been regularly updated. He has gained in color, music, history and fighters. Many talented people have joined me in the adventure to enhance and complement the content... In particular Darvel, who has done an incredible job with music.

In 2019, the game covered so many different sagas and characters that it once again changed its name to become "Dragon Ball Devolution", quite simply.

The death of Flash in 2021 has not changed anything in the project: the game now runs on Ruffle, the Flash emulator, you do not have to worry!
