    Dragon Ball Z Ultimate Power 2

    4.5
    Embed

    Who do you know from the Dragon Ball Z characters? Goku? Vegeta? Gohan? Or Freeza? Whoever you know, you are going to have a lot of fun with this game. The most talented fighters who are skillful and talented from each other are getting on the arena for you. You should call your friend immediately to fight with ultimate powered fighters against each other.

    Game controls:
    Player 1:
    Move: "W, A, S, D"
    Attack: "G"
    Special key: "H"
    Filter key: "J"
    Counter: "T"
    Block: "Y"
    Tag: "U"
    Basic Special: "SPACE" + "D"
    Super Special: "SPACE" + "A"

    Player 2:
    Move: "ARROW KEYS"
    Attack: "1"
    Special key: "2"
    Filter key: "3"
    Counter: "4"
    Block: "5"
    Tag: "6"
    Basic Special: "LEFT ARROW" + "0"
    Super Special: "RIGHT ARROW" + "0"

    Have fun!
