Bleach Vs Naruto 3.5

8/10 - 3549 votes
Played 885 908 times

Action Games Anime Fighting Flash

Bleach Vs Naruto, the star of online crossover fighting games, is back in version 3.5, specifically optimized for better performance. Play as more than 40 characters from your 2 favorite manga series. Fights have never been so intense and fluid. Play alone against the computer or with two players on the same computer. This version 3.5 also adds an exciting new story mode, called "Musou Play". In a beat'em up style, you'll progress across a map and must defeat waves of enemies and formidable bosses while gaining experience and leveling up.

Menu Screen:
Z/S: Up/Down
A/D: Left/Right
J: Select

Game Controls:
A/D: Left/Right
S: Defense
J: Attack
U: Distance attack
K: Jump
L: Dash
I: Special attack
O: Summon assistance
